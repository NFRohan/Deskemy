# Deskemy 2.0

Sorry for the long hiatus. Over the past month I wrapped up my undergrad, started my first job, and I'm currently moving to a new city. I hope to work at a more regular pace once I'm settled in. Please wish me luck in starting this new phase of life.

Deskemy 2.0 is a complete rebuild of the frontend, and a step towards Linux support. The app has a new native interface in place of the
web view, and this release brings a mini player, resources shown next to their
lectures, and a round of player polish. Your library, progress, bookmarks and
settings carry over.

## Highlights

- **Mini player.** Press **T**, or the picture-in-picture button in the player,
  to shrink Deskemy to a small always-on-top window beside your editor or
  terminal. Hover it for play/pause, ±10s, next and the seek bar. Drag it
  anywhere and resize it; it keeps the video's shape and opens where you last
  left it. Double-click, **T** or **Esc** takes you back to the full player.
- **Resources with their lectures.** Exercise PDFs, notes and files can show
  right under the lecture they belong to, on the course page and in the
  player's course panel (Settings → Resources, on by default). Tick them off
  as you finish them.
- **Pause on exercises (optional).** Autoplay can stop at the end of a lecture
  that has resources and offer them before moving on. Off by default
  (Settings → Resources).
- **No WebView2.** Deskemy no longer needs Microsoft Edge WebView2 installed.

## Player

- The player has received significant changes, inspired by the lovely UI from Plezy. Filled control icons, a thicker seek bar, and a darker shade behind the
  controls so they stay readable over bright videos.
- Clicking the video no longer pauses it (double-clicking for fullscreen
  used to pause and resume too). Space or K pauses.
- A back arrow to leave the player; the mouse's back button still works.
- The pointer hides along with the controls, and the controls tuck away when
  the pointer leaves the window.
- The default playback speed now applies to every course, unless you give a
  course its own speed in the player. The speed menu shows when a course has
  one, with **Use default** to drop it. Choosing a default in Settings applies
  it to all courses.

## Library

- Imports: TypeScript `.ts` files are no longer mistaken for videos, and code
  project folders (with `node_modules` and the like) show up as one resource
  instead of hundreds of files. For a course you imported before, add its
  folder again to pick this up — progress and bookmarks are kept.
- The activity graph on Stats shows lectures finished as well as time watched
  when you hover a day or a bar.
- Sharp, ClearType-style text, and Windows-style minimize, maximize and
  close buttons.

## Installing and updating

- **From Deskemy 1.x:** accept the update when it's offered, or install one of
  the files below over it.
- **Setup** (`deskemy_2.0.0_x64-setup.exe`): per-user, no admin needed.
- **MSI** (`deskemy_2.0.0_x64_en-US.msi`): per-machine, in Program Files. It
  upgrades an existing 1.x MSI install in place.
- **Portable** (`Deskemy_2.0.0_x64-portable.zip`): extract and run; everything
  stays in its folder.
- From now on Deskemy checks for updates when it starts and shows a banner.
  Nothing downloads until you click Update. Settings → About has a Check
  button.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Good to know

- The first launch upgrades your library database. Deskemy 1.x can still open
  it afterwards, but a backup exported from 2.0 won't import into 1.x.
- Needs a GPU with OpenGL 3.3 (any integrated GPU from the last decade).
- Windows only for now. Linux is in progress, hopefully in another month.

## Known issues

- The first launch straight from the setup's "Run Deskemy" may open minimized
  or behind other windows. Click Deskemy in the taskbar.
- The mouse's back button works over the video, not over the control bar or
  the title bar.
