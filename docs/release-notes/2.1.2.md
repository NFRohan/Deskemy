# Deskemy 2.1.2

A loop for the parts you need to see twice, links that work in PDFs, and an
importer that understands many more of the ways downloaded courses are laid
out. Also: courses stay in their career tracks when their folder changes.

## New

- **A–B loop.** Replay a stretch of a lecture as many times as you need:
  press **A** (or the new loop button by the speed) where it starts, and
  again where it ends. It loops from there until you press it a third time.
  - The stretch is highlighted on the seek bar, and the button shows its
    times while it's set ("0:40 – 1:15").
  - Slow it down with the speed control while it loops.
  - It belongs to the lecture: the next one starts without a loop.
- **Links in PDFs work.** A web link opens in your browser, and a link to
  another page (a table of contents) scrolls there. Pointing at a link shows
  where it goes, in the corner. Only web and email links open: never a
  file or program a PDF links to.

## Better imports

We ran Deskemy's importer over a few dozen real course downloads and fixed
everything it got wrong:

- **Subtitles named the way downloaders name them** are now attached to
  their lecture, so their text shows up in search: `Lecture_en.srt`,
  `Lecture English.vtt`, `Lecture.en_US.vtt`, and subtitles whose name is
  spelled a little differently from the video's.
- **One language per lecture in search.** A lecture with 17 subtitle
  languages showed every sentence 17 times in the results; now it's
  indexed once (the plain one, else English).
- **Courses wrapped in an extra folder** (`~Get Your Files Here !`, or a
  folder of the course's own name beside a promo video) are unwrapped. The
  course keeps its real sections and its own title.
- **A folder per lecture** (`2-AWS/1. Why Terraform/Why Terraform.mp4`) is
  read as one course with sections, not split into several courses of one
  lecture each. Each lecture is named and numbered after its folder, and the
  notes in its folder go with it.
- **Download sites' adverts are left out.** `[SiteName].url` links and
  "Get Bonus Downloads Here" files no longer pile up, a hundred per course,
  among the lectures' resources. A `[SiteName]` tag at the front of a
  folder's name is left off the course's title.
- **Mangled names are tidied:** `Module_1_Introduction_to_Linux` becomes
  "Module 1 Introduction to Linux", a `?` or `:` a downloader turned into
  `_` reads properly again, and double spaces are closed up.
- **A download that isn't extracted yet** (a folder of `.zip` or `.rar`
  parts) now says so, instead of "No playable video files found".

**Courses already in your library keep their old layout** until they're
imported again. To refresh one, add its folder again with **Add Folder** (or
drop it on the window): your progress, bookmarks, ticked-off resources,
tags and career tracks are kept.

## Fixes

- **Courses stay in their career tracks** (#14). Editing a file in a
  course's folder with *Auto-rescan folders* on re-imported the course, and
  that took it out of every career track it was in. It also quietly reset
  the course's saved speed, subtitle language and audio track. A rescan (or
  adding the folder again) now keeps all of those.

  Thanks to @feliceforgione for the clear report, with the steps to
  reproduce it and the detail that progress survived, which pointed straight
  at the cause.
- **Clicks don't go through the PDF viewer.** Clicking a small PDF's page,
  or the space around it, could click the course page underneath: the cover
  picker opened, or a lecture started playing.

## Installing and updating

- **From 2.x or 1.x:** accept the update when it's offered, or install one
  of the files below over it.
- **Setup** (`deskemy_2.1.2_x64-setup.exe`): per-user, no admin needed.
- **MSI** (`deskemy_2.1.2_x64_en-US.msi`): per-machine, in Program Files.
- **Portable** (`Deskemy_2.1.2_x64-portable.zip`): extract and run;
  everything stays in its folder.

Nothing changes in your library's database. Checksums are in
`SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Linux (beta)

- **Fedora, openSUSE:** `deskemy_2.1.2_x86_64.rpm`
- **Ubuntu, Debian, Mint:** `deskemy_2.1.2_amd64.deb`
- **Any distro:** `Deskemy_2.1.2_x86_64.AppImage` (make it executable
  first). It updates itself; the .deb and .rpm install over the old version.

It needs a distro from 2024 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04+, Mint 22+, Debian 13+,
Fedora 40+, openSUSE Tumbleweed). See [2.1.1's notes](https://github.com/NFRohan/Deskemy/releases/tag/v2.1.1)
for the details, and tell us how it runs in #7.
