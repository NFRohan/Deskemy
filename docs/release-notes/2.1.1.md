# Deskemy 2.1.1

Search that keeps up as you type and sorts what it finds, resources that can
pop up before a lecture ends, bookmarks on the seek bar, and skips that land
where you send them.

## New

- **Resources can pop up before a lecture ends.** With *Pause on exercises
  and resources* on, the new **Resource popup timing** setting chooses when a
  lecture's resources appear: *at the end* (as before), or *10 s*, *30 s* or
  *1 min before*.
  - Early, they show in a corner card while the lecture keeps playing. Open
    one (a PDF pauses the video, as before), tick it off, or dismiss the card.
  - On a short lecture they never appear before its halfway point.
  - Seeking back before that point puts the card away; reaching it again
    brings it back. The mini player shows a chip at the same moment.

  Thanks to @dcsm8 for the suggestion.
- **No stop for resources you've already done.** If every resource of a
  lecture is ticked off, autoplay goes straight on at its end.
- **Bookmarks on the seek bar.** Each bookmark is a small amber mark on the
  bar. Point at one to see its label; click it to jump there.
- **Search results are grouped.** Courses, sections, lectures and resources
  each get their own group, with a count, before the matches in spoken
  subtitle text. A long group shows its first few and a *Show all* link.
  - Each kind is searched on its own, so dozens of files called `notes.pdf`
    no longer crowd the sections and lectures out of the results.
  - Lectures and resources say which section they're in, so lectures with
    the same name can be told apart.
  - **A section** opens its course with that section expanded and in view.
  - **A resource** opens straight away, as from the course page: a PDF in the
    viewer, anything else in its own app.
- **The player's title bar shows the section:** Library / course / section /
  lecture. In a narrow window the longest names shorten first, so the
  section stays readable.

## Fixes

- **Search follows what you type.** Results only updated when you pressed
  Enter, and clearing the box left the old results on screen. They now
  update as you type, and clear with the box.
- **Skips land where you send them.** The skip buttons and J / L (10 s) and
  the arrow keys (5 s) landed on the nearest keyframe, which could be seconds
  off, so a skip sometimes barely moved or overshot. They're exact now.
- **The seek bar doesn't jump back after a seek.** Clicking the bar, a skip
  or a bookmark could flick the bar back to the old position for a moment
  before it caught up.
- **The seek bar glides** instead of moving in small steps, and stops
  exactly when you pause.

  Thanks to @l0udsil3nc3, whose work on running Deskemy on Linux (#10)
  prompted a closer look at seeking.
- **Continue Watching keeps its picture.** A lecture with a long name pushed
  the card's image down to a thin strip.
- **A clearer message when the video engine can't load.** Instead of only
  "libmpv is not available", the error now says why (for example, which
  library it needs is missing).

## Installing and updating

- **From 2.x or 1.x:** accept the update when it's offered, or install one
  of the files below over it.
- **Setup** (`deskemy_2.1.1_x64-setup.exe`): per-user, no admin needed.
- **MSI** (`deskemy_2.1.1_x64_en-US.msi`): per-machine, in Program Files.
- **Portable** (`Deskemy_2.1.1_x64-portable.zip`): extract and run;
  everything stays in its folder.

Nothing changes in your library's database this time. Checksums are in
`SHA256SUMS.txt`.
