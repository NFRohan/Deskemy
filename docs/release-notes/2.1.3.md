# Deskemy 2.1.3

Career tracks become roadmaps, the course page gets a new look, and most of
the wish list in #13 is in: the mouse's back button, an open-folder button,
a "Hide finished" filter, tag suggestions, course websites and track header
images.

## Career tracks are roadmaps

A track is a path through its courses, and its page now reads as one.

- **The roadmap.** Each course is a step down a line: a tick once it's
  done, its number until then. Each card shows the course's cover, where
  it stands (*Up next*, *In progress*, *Completed*, *Not started*), how
  long it is and how far you are. The **⋮** on a card moves it earlier or
  later, or takes it out of the track.
- **Next up.** The course to work on and the lecture you'll pick up at,
  with a **Continue** button that goes straight into it, and how much
  time is left in that course. A finished track says so instead.
- **Your progress.** The percentage, plus courses done, lectures left and
  roughly how much watching is left in the whole track.
- **A header image** for a track, from **⋮ → Add a header image**. It shows
  across the top of the track and on its card in the list. A track without
  one gets a gradient of its own.
- **Say what a track is for:** the description sits under its name.
- **Most recently used first.** The tracks list (and the library's track
  filter) puts the track you last opened or created at the top.

In a wide window the roadmap sits beside *Next up* and *Your progress*; in
a narrow one they stack.

## A new course page

- **The header** keeps the cover, title and tags together, with a bigger
  **Resume** button that names the lecture it opens, and the progress bar
  underneath. Changing the cover and removing the course are in the **⋮**
  menu.
- **Sections** show a ring of how much of them is done ("3/4", a tick when
  finished), their number, and "4 lectures · 45m".
- **Lectures** are easier to scan: larger titles, a filled tick when done
  (no more strikethrough), and *Up next* marked down the row's edge.
  Resources under a lecture are set apart with a file badge and their type.

## More from #13

- **The mouse's back button** goes back on every page, as in a browser:
  back from a course you opened from search returns to the search. An open
  dialog closes first.
- **Open the course's folder** with the folder button next to the star.
- **Select and copy a course's title.**
- **Hide finished** courses from the library with the toggle after the
  filters. It's remembered; the *Finished* filter still shows them.
- **Tag suggestions.** Typing a tag offers the ones you already use, so
  "devops" doesn't become a second "DevOps".
- **A course's website.** Add the page you bought it from under the tags;
  it opens in your browser. Only web addresses are accepted. It's kept when
  the course is rescanned.

Thanks again to @feliceforgione for the list. Notes on courses and tracks
are next.

## Fixes

- **The tag field lets go of the keyboard.** Once clicked it kept every
  key until you left the page. Now **Esc** leaves it (and clears it), and
  so does a click anywhere else on the page.

## Installing and updating

- **From 2.x or 1.x:** accept the update when it's offered, or install one
  of the files below over it.
- **Setup** (`deskemy_2.1.3_x64-setup.exe`): per-user, no admin needed.
- **MSI** (`deskemy_2.1.3_x64_en-US.msi`): per-machine, in Program Files.
- **Portable** (`Deskemy_2.1.3_x64-portable.zip`): extract and run;
  everything stays in its folder.

Your library's database gains room for course websites and track header
images the first time 2.1.3 opens it. Nothing in it changes, and 2.1.2 can
still open it if you go back. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Linux (beta)

- **Fedora, openSUSE:** `deskemy_2.1.3_x86_64.rpm`
- **Ubuntu, Debian, Mint:** `deskemy_2.1.3_amd64.deb`
- **Any distro:** `Deskemy_2.1.3_x86_64.AppImage` (make it executable
  first). It updates itself; the .deb and .rpm install over the old version.

It needs a distro from 2024 or newer (Ubuntu 24.04+, Mint 22+, Debian 13+,
Fedora 40+, openSUSE Tumbleweed). See [2.1.1's notes](https://github.com/NFRohan/Deskemy/releases/tag/v2.1.1)
for the details, and tell us how it runs in #7.
