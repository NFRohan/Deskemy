# Deskemy 2.0.2

Fixes for things you reported, and a few small improvements.

## Fixes

- **Your place in a lecture is kept.** Progress could sporadically jump back
  to the start: leaving the player (or opening a lecture from a slow drive)
  could save the wrong position over the real one. Fixed — a lecture now
  always resumes where you left it.
- **The first launch after installing opens in front** instead of behind
  other windows (it looked minimized).
- **The Chapters button stays.** It could disappear partway through a video
  with chapters (#6).
- **The window across monitors with different scaling.** Dragging the full
  window (not only the mini player) onto a monitor with another scale could
  make it jump or resize on its own. It now crosses over the way the mini
  player does since 2.0.1. Thanks again to @ihor-sokoliuk (#8).

## Improvements

- **Resources in lecture order.** With "Keep videos and resources together",
  numbered articles and files now sit between the lectures around them —
  `003 Configuring Git` between lectures 2 and 4 — instead of all at the end
  of the section. "Pause on exercises and resources" offers them at the end
  of the lecture they follow.
- **Mouse back button anywhere in the player** — over the video, the control
  bar, the title bar or the mini player.
- **Mini player: choose where the full player comes back.** Settings →
  Playback → "Open the full player where the mini player is": on (the
  default) opens it on the monitor the mini player is on; off brings it back
  where it was.
- **Snap Layouts** (Windows 11): rest the pointer on the maximize button to
  pick a layout, as with other apps.
- **Crisper player icons** in the control bar.
- **Career tracks** name a course's status (not started / in progress /
  completed) when you hover its icon.

## Installing and updating

- **From 2.0.x or 1.x:** accept the update when it's offered, or install one of
  the files below over it.
- **Setup** (`deskemy_2.0.2_x64-setup.exe`): per-user, no admin needed.
- **MSI** (`deskemy_2.0.2_x64_en-US.msi`): per-machine, in Program Files.
- **Portable** (`Deskemy_2.0.2_x64-portable.zip`): extract and run; everything
  stays in its folder.

Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
