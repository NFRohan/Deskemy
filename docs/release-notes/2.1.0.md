# Deskemy 2.1.0

A PDF viewer built in, drag & drop import, resources you can pair with any
lecture, and subtitles that keep their language from one lecture to the
next. Plus a round of fixes, several of them from your reports.

## New

- **A built-in PDF viewer.** The PDFs that come with a course open right in
  Deskemy: from the course page, the player's Resources panel or the card at
  the end of a lecture. Scroll through the pages, zoom (the buttons, or
  Ctrl + / − / 0), see page n of m, and use the keys: PgUp / PgDn,
  Home / End, the arrows.
  - **Mark as done** from the viewer's toolbar ticks the resource off where
    you opened it.
  - Opened over the player, it pauses the video, leaving fullscreen or the
    mini player first. Close it (Back, Esc or the mouse's back button) and
    you're back at the paused lecture, or at the end-of-lecture card if you
    came from there.
  - **Open in your PDF app** is one click away if you'd rather use your own.
  - A damaged or password-protected PDF says so instead of failing quietly.

  It runs on PDFium, Chrome's PDF engine, in the build without JavaScript.
  Thanks to @dcsm8 for the suggestion.
- **Drag & drop import.** Drop a course folder, a folder full of courses, or
  several folders onto the library:
  - **One course** opens the usual import preview.
  - **Several** open a list to tick. Courses already in your library start
    unticked. They're imported one after another, with progress per course.
    You can hide the progress (the sidebar button brings it back) or stop
    after the current course.
  - **A list from one folder** also offers to import that folder as a single
    course instead.
- **Add Folder recognises a folder of courses.** Picking a folder that holds
  several courses now imports them as separate courses, instead of one big
  course with each course as a section. A folder counts as a course with
  sections when two or more of its subfolders hold videos, and there are
  more of those folders than videos beside them. If a folder is read the
  wrong way, drop the folders you want onto the library instead.
- **Pair a resource with a lecture.** Hover a resource on the course page and
  click its link button. You can pair it with any lecture in the course, with
  no lecture, or set it back to automatic. Type to filter the lectures by
  their name or their section's.
  - A pairing applies everywhere: the course page, the player's Resources
    panel and the end-of-lecture pause.
  - It's kept through re-imports, auto-rescan and Locate folder.
- **Subtitle text is searchable as soon as a course is imported.** Before,
  you had to run Settings → Index subtitle text first. The button is still
  there to rebuild the whole index.

## Fixes

- **The subtitle language stays the same from lecture to lecture (#11).**
  Pick Portuguese in one lecture and the next one could show French, or no
  subtitles at all. Deskemy remembered the subtitle by its position in the
  track list, and that list differs from file to file.
  - It now remembers the language (and the track's title). Each new lecture
    finds the same language among its own tracks.
  - Regional variants and both kinds of language code count as the same
    language: `pt`, `pt-BR` and `por` all match. All 184 ISO 639-2 languages
    are covered.
  - A lecture without that language shows no subtitles, and the next lecture
    that has it shows them again.
  - A choice saved by 2.0 is carried over the first time you play the course.

  Thanks to @FilipeGBC for reporting it.
- **A lecture that ends stays on its last frame.** When playback stopped at
  the end of a lecture (autoplay off, resources offered, or the sleep timer),
  the player could show 0:00 / −0:00, lose its subtitle button, go black when
  you opened a panel, and Play started the lecture over. Now the lecture
  stays on its last frame with its controls working.
  - **Play moves on to the next lecture**, as autoplay would have. On a
    course's last lecture, Play watches it again.
  - Seek back to rewatch the end.
- **Menus close when the lecture changes.** The subtitles, speed, audio,
  chapters or sleep menu could stay open into the next lecture, even into
  another course, and pop up again later.
- **The subtitle button no longer looks stuck open.** It was highlighted
  whenever subtitles were on, the same as while its menu is open. Now a small
  underline shows that subtitles are on, and the highlight only means the
  menu is open.
- **Lectures in very deep folders play.** A video whose full path was over
  260 characters was imported as unplayable, and a resource in such a
  folder didn't open. Both work now.
- **One copy of Deskemy per library.** Two copies open on the same library
  could each overwrite what the other saved. Launching Deskemy again now
  brings the open window forward, restoring it if it was minimised. The
  installed app, a portable copy and other data folders stay separate.
- **Add Folder no longer does nothing when the folder picker can't open
  (#10).** On a Linux desktop without a working file-chooser service, Add
  Folder, Locate folder, choosing a cover, and backup export / import all
  looked dead. They now say what's missing. Every dialog that closes without
  a choice is logged. Linux builds aren't released yet, but some of you are
  already building Deskemy there. Thanks to @l0udsil3nc3 for the detailed
  report and diagnosis.
- **Locate folder keeps subtitle search working.** After moving a course and
  locating its folder, rebuilding the subtitle index lost that course's
  subtitles.
- **Course header at the narrowest window width:** "N resources done" no
  longer runs into the course's total duration.

## Improvements

- **Coming back from a video, you're where you left off.** The course page
  and the library used to jump back to the top every time you left the
  player, from the window or the mini player. They now keep their scroll
  position. Opening a course starts at its top, and a new search, sort or
  filter starts the library at the top.
- **Mini player: back to the full player beside close.** The button sat in
  the top-left corner, right where you grab the window to resize it. It now
  sits beside close at the top right. Both buttons are set in from the edges,
  so the corners are free for resizing.

## Installing and updating

- **From 2.0.x or 1.x:** accept the update when it's offered, or install one
  of the files below over it.
- **Setup** (`deskemy_2.1.0_x64-setup.exe`): per-user, no admin needed.
- **MSI** (`deskemy_2.1.0_x64_en-US.msi`): per-machine, in Program Files.
- **Portable** (`Deskemy_2.1.0_x64-portable.zip`): extract and run;
  everything stays in its folder. It now ships with `pdfium.dll` for the PDF
  viewer. Keep it beside `deskemy.exe`, or PDFs will open in your PDF app
  instead.

The first launch updates your library's database for resource pairings and
the remembered subtitle language. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
